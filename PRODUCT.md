# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Women who buy ready-made strip lashes to apply themselves: an everyday light look, or a fuller set
for weddings, parties and evenings out. The shop serves both the general public and the clients of
the Serag lash studio, who already trust the brand's lash maps and buy the strips between visits.
They shop on their phones, mostly in Arabic, also in Hebrew and English.

## Product Purpose

Serag Lashes is an online shop for daily and occasion lashes, care tools and gift cards, with
delivery across Israel. Success is a visitor who understands within seconds which lash suits her
day, picks it with confidence and checks out (card, Apple Pay, Bit or cash), and comes back for
the next pair.

## Positioning

The six lash designs come from the lash maps drawn in the Serag studio for real eyes: each strip is
a studio-made map she can apply herself in minutes and remove whenever she likes. The name means a
lamp: a quiet light that reveals beauty without overpowering it.

## Operating Context

- Online only. No walk-in shop; delivery across Israel.
- Trilingual: Arabic (primary, right to left), Hebrew (right to left), English.
- Loyalty points on every order; gift cards; WhatsApp is a live support and ordering channel.
- Built in Next.js (app router, `app/[lang]`), with an admin for catalog, orders and offers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Catalog of products with options (variants), sale prices, stock, offers and gift cards.
- Accounts by phone, favorites, addresses, order tracking, reviews after delivery.
- Payment by card, Apple Pay, Bit or cash; delivery zones and free-delivery threshold set in admin.
- Prices in shekels (₪); everything must work right to left.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Serag Lashes (سراج لاشز). The lamp/light meaning is the brand's story.
- The owner has a finished logo (not yet in the repository; the current monogram is a stand-in).

## Evidence on Hand

- The owner has real product photographs, photographs of models/clients wearing the lashes (with
  permission to use) and the logo file. None are in the repository yet: until they are uploaded,
  pages use the drawn lash boxes and clearly listed stand-ins, never presented as Serag's own work.
- No reviews, press or sales figures are on hand; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Show the lash on the eye: every choice is made by seeing how it looks, not by reading about it.
2. Arabic first: the right-to-left reading and Arabic type lead; Hebrew and English follow the same system.
3. Phone first: the purchase must be comfortable one-handed on a phone.
4. Studio craft, honestly: the studio-map story is the difference; no invented claims or social proof.
