# Storefront (home, shop, product, cart)

Scope: the whole storefront under app/[lang] (home is the lead surface). Visitor mode: Persuade (home), Operate (shop, product, cart, checkout).
Audience: women buying strip lashes to apply themselves, Arabic first, on phones. Action: add a lash to the bag.
Proof on hand: real product and model photographs and the logo exist with the owner, not yet uploaded; until then drawn lash boxes and stand-ins.

## Direction contract

FORM: kohl jar and mirwad (Arab kohl ritual) — seed:ffa53d47aa3d5540, assigned; palette dealt by taste-skill (steel-orange), type Albert Sans + a calm Arabic sans.
THESIS: lashes are chosen the way kohl is applied: on the eye, one deliberate stroke. Refuses the pink-nude beauty template and the black-and-gold luxury opposite.
OWN-WORLD: stone ground #eeeeeb with a deeper stone surface, kohl ink #1c1d1f for type and lines, one copper accent #c2501b (the makhala) for the single primary action and active states. Hairline kohl strokes as the line system; square photographs; flat grounds, no gradients, no glows.
STORY: see the lash on an eye, read three plain facts (style, length, occasion), add it to the bag.
FIRST VIEWPORT: the product on the eye at full width with the headline set beside it, one kohl stroke drawing once under the headline, price and Add to bag visible in the hero.
SIGNATURE: the kohl stroke — a tapered line drawn once along a lash line on load and on hover of a product, annotated with leader-line call-outs (style · length · occasion).
RAISES: from the street-magazine challenger, every product picture carries leader-line call-outs bound to real attributes; from ebru, motion is one continuous pass, never scattered effects.
